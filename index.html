Who we are

Since 2019 we have been shaping an exceptional coffee experience, born from a passion for detail and quality.

Our journey began in the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia, where we built our presence across five branches: two cafés, two drive-thrus and one in-house service branch, close to our guests wherever their day takes them.

In 2026 we took a strategic step and opened our first international branch in Chicago, carrying the authentic Saudi coffee experience to the world.
